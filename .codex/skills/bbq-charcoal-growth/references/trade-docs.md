# Trade Docs And Sales Assets

## Purpose

Support sales with drafts based on inquiry data and reusable product/factory facts. Do not send or finalize official trade documents without human approval.

## Inputs

Collect or infer cautiously:

- buyer company
- buyer email
- country
- destination port
- product
- quantity
- packaging requirement
- incoterm
- target specification
- requested documents
- timeline

## Draftable Assets

- first reply email
- sample arrangement email
- follow-up sequence
- product specification sheet
- factory inspection summary
- indicative quotation draft
- proforma invoice draft
- commercial invoice draft
- packing list draft

## Draft Rules

- Mark price, availability, lead time, freight, and packing as `subject to confirmation`.
- Ask for destination port and packaging before quoting.
- Reuse factory evidence only within documented boundaries.
- Use clear buyer-facing English.
- Keep the first response short; ask for the missing fields needed to quote.

## First Reply Template Shape

```text
Thank you for your inquiry.

We understand you are looking for [product] for [country/market], around [quantity].

To prepare the right quotation basis, please confirm:
- destination port
- preferred packing size
- target specification or burn requirement
- trial order or repeat monthly volume
- required documents

We can also share a factory inspection summary and product specification for your review.
```
