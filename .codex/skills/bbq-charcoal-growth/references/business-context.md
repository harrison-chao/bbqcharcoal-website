# Business Context

## Positioning

BBQ Charcoal Export is a B2B export inquiry website for overseas importers, distributors, restaurant groups, and private-label buyers sourcing BBQ charcoal from Malaysia.

Use this core positioning:

> A Malaysia BBQ briquette charcoal factory with documented third-party inspection evidence, 800 MT monthly capacity, OEM packaging, and export support for importers, distributors, restaurants, and private-label buyers.

## Evidence Assets

Use these facts as trust anchors:

- Control Union (Malaysia) factory inspection date: 2025-10-16.
- Inspection job: `CUMAL/1880/25(10)`.
- Documented product line: briquette charcoal.
- Maximum documented capacity: 800 MT/month.
- Equipment: 12 briquette machines and 72 carbonization kilns.
- Facility: 82,146 sq ft factory building area and 26,000 sq ft storage area.
- Weighing: 80 MT weighbridge.
- Certification evidence: PEFC CoC `CSIM-PEFC-CoC-0020`, valid until 2027-07-18.
- Quality controls: raw material weighing, daily production records, outgoing goods records, moisture testing, weight/density checks, metal detection, and SGS laboratory testing several times per year.

## Product Claim Boundaries

- Treat BBQ briquettes as the primary factory-backed product.
- Treat lump charcoal and binchotan as sourcing options unless current factory/product evidence says otherwise.
- Do not imply all product categories share the same inspected capacity.
- Do not invent certifications, lab results, prices, or lead times.
- Mark prices as indicative or subject to confirmation unless explicitly approved.

## Target Buyer Segments

- Importers and wholesalers: need stable supply, documentation, container logistics.
- Restaurant groups: need consistent heat, burn behavior, low ash, repeat supply.
- Private-label retail buyers: need OEM packaging, label support, cartons/bags, documentation.
- Middle East buyers: often care about documents, packaging, seasonal demand, port routing, and certifications.
