# SEO/GEO Content Standards

## Purpose

Articles must earn search visibility, build supplier trust, and assist sales conversion. Do not publish generic charcoal content.

## Required Article Brief

Each brief must include:

- Primary keyword
- Secondary keywords
- Search intent
- Target buyer
- Core answer
- Required factory facts
- Outline
- Required table or checklist
- FAQ list
- Internal links
- CTA
- Schema recommendation
- Do-not-say list

## Required Article Structure

1. TL;DR answer, 80-120 words.
2. Buyer context.
3. H2 sections that each answer one buyer question.
4. Evidence section using documented factory facts.
5. Comparison table or buyer checklist.
6. FAQ with 5-8 entries.
7. CTA to `/contact` or a relevant landing page.

## Hard Quality Metrics

- 1,500-2,500 words for full articles.
- At least 6 H2 sections.
- At least 1 table.
- At least 1 buyer checklist.
- At least 5 FAQ questions.
- At least 3 internal links.
- Include `/factory-inspection`, `/products`, and `/contact` or relevant landing page.
- Meta title under 60 characters when possible.
- Meta description under 155 characters when possible.
- Slug should be short, lowercase, and hyphenated.

## GEO Rules

Write concise answer blocks that AI systems can quote:

- Define terms clearly.
- Put direct answers near the top.
- Use specific facts and dates.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Prefer lists, tables, and explicit comparisons.

## Priority Topic Clusters

- Supplier evaluation.
- Product specifications.
- Country/market buyer guides.
- OEM/private-label packaging.
- Factory trust and inspection evidence.
